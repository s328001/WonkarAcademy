Frequently asked questions

Who can join the academy?

We coach beginners, amateurs and corporate players. If you are starting from scratch, we begin with the basics: grip, stance, strokes and rules.

Is the trial class really free?

Yes. You can book a free trial class to see the coaching before you decide to join.

How do I apply for coaching?

Fill in the Apply for coaching form with the player's details and preferred batch. It is sent to the academy by email, and we will contact you.

What are the batch timings?

The batches are listed in Batch timings. Please confirm the timing for your chosen batch with us before you join.

How do I pay the fees?

Choose your plan in Pay fees and pay by UPI using GPay, PhonePe or Paytm on your phone. After paying, send us a screenshot on WhatsApp so we can confirm it.

I have another question. How do I reach you?

Call or WhatsApp us, or visit during our opening hours. You can find the number, hours and address in Contact.
